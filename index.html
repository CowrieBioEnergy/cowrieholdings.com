Our Philosophy

A Legacy Symbol.
A Future-Forward
Mandate.

Named after the cowrie shell — once traded as currency across Africa, Asia and Oceania — Cowrie Holdings embodies the power and possibility of patient, purposeful capital. Though little in size, the cowrie shell moved world economies and became a visual mainstay in cultures around the world.

At Cowrie, we believe a little capital can go a long way and have transformative returns. As seed and angel investors, we infuse capital at critical early stages of the venture journey — walking alongside our founders, removing roadblocks as they grow their businesses, teams, and impact.

Sub-Saharan Africa holds a wealth of raw materials, a young and enterprising population, and founders who are increasingly globally connected and deeply knowledgeable about local challenges. We find, equip, and fund the promising founders best positioned to tackle structural challenges with unique solutions.

Investment Thesis

We Back Founders
with the 3 Ps.

As Cowrie invests, we create an ecosystem of problem-solvers who can advance their solutions in parallel. We look for portfolio companies whose founders share what we call the Three Ps — our north star for every investment decision.

P
Proximity

Lived experience — geographic or social familiarity with the fundamental challenge. The ability to experience their impact in real-time, as it unfolds on the ground.

P
Perspective

An ability to look at the problem from multiple angles, identify viable solutions, and understand what needs to happen next to crack it wide open.

P
Pursuit

A dedication to seeing possibility amid a lack of resources — and the willingness to test, iterate, and scale tactical solutions that transcend borders.
